HOW TO PLAY

1. RAM Energy: Each turn you draw 4 cards and your RAM recharges. Playing cards costs RAM.

2. Variable X: Set the variable with let x = 8; or double it with x *= 2;. Boost x further with if (x > 5) — but only if x is above 5!

3. Attack: ATTACK(x) deals damage equal to x (default 4). PURGE() converts your Block into damage.

4. Loops: Multiply your next attack: for(2x Loop) doubles it, PARALLEL() triples it!

5. Defense: DEFENSE(8) gains Block, REBOOT() heals HP, and OVERCLOCK() grants +2 max RAM.

6. End Turn: Click "EXECUTE TURN" to end your turn. The enemy strikes back!
